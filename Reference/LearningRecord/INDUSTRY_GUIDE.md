| Field | Value |
|---|---|

| **Title** | ISGOTT — International Safety Guide for Oil Tankers and Terminals |
| **Category** | Industry Standards & Publications |
| **Description** | ISGOTT (6th edition) is the definitive safety manual for crude and product tanker operations and their interface with shore terminals. Published jointly by ICS, OCIMF, and IAPH, it is not an IMO convention but is so widely adopted that it is treated as mandatory by major charterers and terminal operators. Understanding ISGOTT is essential for any officer involved in tanker cargo operations, and it is central to SIRE 2.0 vetting inspections. |
