### Season 1 — Fishing Vessel Safety Convention (order: 1)
> Context: Covers the history of the Convention, the structural and safety standards it imposes, and how life-saving and fire safety requirements apply to regulated fishing vessels.

| # | Title | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | History and Scope | The 1977 Torremolinos Convention and its 1993 Protocol (which actually entered force). Covers fishing vessels 24m and over on international voyages. Why fishing fleets were excluded from SOLAS and needed their own instrument. |
| 2 | Construction and Safety Standards | The Torremolinos Protocol requirements: structural standards, subdivision, stability, machinery. How PSC applies these standards to fishing vessels. |
| 3 | Life-Saving and Fire Safety | LSA requirements for fishing vessels: liferafts, immersion suits, distress signals. Fire safety standards. The link to STCW-F training requirements. |
